## Preface

_Computational Arts Critical Papers_ is a student-led publication created by the students of the Computational Arts MA and MFA programme at Goldsmiths. It expands the pedagogical scope of the course by offering postgraduate students a public platform through which to enter academic debate and develop their critical voice.

Moving beyond the institutionalised formulas of practice-based research, the publication aims to create a space in which open-ended enquiry and untamed curiosity can unfold, and new questions about contemporary technology, society, and artistic practice can be articulated.

The peculiarity of computation, at once an infrastructure, a methodology, a tool, and an artistic material, introduces a paradigmatic shift; a distribution of the sensible in which the distinction between maker and user evaporates – even as decentralisation rapidly gives way to new forms of totalisation and control – and art’s poetic playfulness and public good converge. This shift demands repositioning not only aesthetic frameworks, but also political ones; giving art a crucial role in the elaboration of the present.

As those who learn but are not machines, as those whose experience cannot be reduced to input and whose artistic experimentation cannot be simply encoded as output, Goldsmiths’ Computational Arts students take these questions in their own hands. Written, designed, and edited by students, and developed through an internal peer-review process, _Computational Arts Critical Papers_ collects emerging questions and experimental methodologies in Computational Arts. Through their contributions, the students probe the tensions and possibilities of computation to find its dimensions, helping to shape the field from the ground up.
