## Preface

Computational Arts Critical Papers is a student-led publication. Its aim is to provide Goldsmiths’ Computational Arts MA and MFA students with the opportunity to enter the academic debate and test their voice in public. Beyond the institutionalised formula of practice-based research, it offers our postgraduate students a space in which open-ended research and untamed curiosity can unfold, and a platform through which the critical elaboration of new questions about our present can be articulated.

The peculiarity of computation -at once an infrastructure, a methodology, a tool, and an artistic material- introduces an age in which the distinction between maker and user evaporates, even as decentralisation rapidly gives way to new forms of re-normalisation. It is also an age in which art’s poetic playfulness and public good coincide; in which the accepted categories of art, design, production, science, and critical thinking intersect and merge, without a vantage point or stable authority from which to speak - a new distribution of the sensible that demands not only a renewed aesthetic positioning, but also a new political one.

As those who learn but are not machines; as those whose experience cannot be reduced to input and whose artistic experimentation cannot be encoded as output, Goldsmiths’ Computational Arts students at take these questions in their own hands. They probe at computation to identify its dimensions, tensions, and possibilities, and in doing so contribute to shape the field of investigation from the ground up.

- CACP is designed and edited by the students of the Computational Arts MA and MFA programme. All contributions have undergone an internal peer-review process.

Dr Mattia Paganelli, MA/MFA Research & Theory Associate
